Statue Duty privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Statue Duty is a small game about protecting a statue from pigeons. This page explains what happens with your information when you play it.

What the game stores

The game saves your high scores, the dates of your best runs, and whether sound is turned on. This is stored only on your own device. It is never sent to us, and you can delete it by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the game.

What the game does not do

Statue Duty has no accounts, does not ask for your name or email address, does not use analytics or tracking, does not show ads, and does not share or sell any information.

Services the game uses

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your device’s IP address when the game loads, and they may keep technical logs under their own privacy policies: GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game changes how it handles information, for example by adding ads, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact email on the Statue Duty page in the Google Play Store.
